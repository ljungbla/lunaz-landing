Questions, answered

The honest answers.

Do I need to believe in astrology?

No. Plenty of people use Lunaz to reflect, journal and get to know themselves. Take what resonates and leave the rest.

Is Lunaz therapy?

No. Lunaz is a wellness and educational companion, not therapy or medical care. If you need more support, Lumi will point you toward real people who can help.

Are the readings made up?

They're calculated. Planetary positions for your astrology and Human Design are checked against NASA JPL Horizons data.

Can I just vent?

Yes. Talk about your day, ask an open question, or think out loud. No reading required.

Is Lumi a real person?

Lumi is AI, and always tells you so.

Do I need my exact birth time?

Only for your rising sign and your Human Design. Everything else works with your birth date.

How do credits work?

A short message is 1 credit, a longer reply is 2, and a full reading is 8. Free accounts get 20 credits every day.
